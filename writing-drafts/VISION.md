# Writing Vision

## What this is

A place to draft long-form writing about technology, AI, crypto, and the future of building software. The writing here is meant to be opinionated, personal, and grounded in real experience. No corporate fluff.

## Voice

- First person, conversational, direct
- Strong opinions backed by firsthand evidence
- Technical enough to be credible, accessible enough to be interesting
- Humor is dry, not forced
- Stories and lived experience over abstract theorizing
- Short paragraphs, liberal use of headers
- When something is bullshit, say so

## Themes

- The collision of AI and software engineering
- How tools shape the people who use them (and vice versa)
- Crypto, programmable money, and economic mechanism design
- Building things that work vs. building things that sound impressive
- The gap between how people talk about technology and how it actually gets used

## Process

Each piece gets its own subfolder. Drafts live here until they're ready to publish. Supporting research, notes, and outlines can live alongside the main piece.
